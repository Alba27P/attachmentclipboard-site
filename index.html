Attachment Clipboard for Gmail™

Pass an attachment you received on to someone else without saving it to your device first.

How it works

  • Hover an attachment in an open email and choose Hold.
  • Open a new message or reply, expand the clipboard in the corner of Gmail, and choose Attach.
  • Held files delete themselves after the time you set, from 15 minutes to 24 hours, or when you close the browser.

Kept in your browser

Held files are encrypted in your browser's own storage. They are never uploaded to a server, and there is no account or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
